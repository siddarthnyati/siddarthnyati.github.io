About me

Six years of product management across payments, healthcare and tax. Now I ship AI agents into production.

Currently Product Manager, AI Agents and Platform, at Visa.

My journey

Engineering at VIT, Vellore. Four years writing software before I knew the word product. The habit I kept: if I can describe it, I can build it.

Infosys: India's tax portal. India's tax portal: 600K people could dispute an overcharge online instead of queueing at an office.

Dartmouth: engineering management. A master's degree, and a student loan that taught me how credit really works. It later became DeFiLend.

Motional: autonomous vehicles. Autonomous vehicles. If a rider does not trust the car the first time, there is no second time.

ResMed: healthcare platforms. Zero-touch resupply, then a document platform where a model reads the faxes and a person checks the doubtful ones.

Visa: payments, and agents. Tokenization, 3DS and passkeys, and the agent work: a release-comms pipeline and a seven-gate lifecycle 50+ product managers now run.
